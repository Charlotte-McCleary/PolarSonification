Trignometric Data Sonification

Circle: r = 1

A circle is a mathematical function that looks like a smooth, close curve. It consists of points in a plane that are the same distance from a single fixed point known as the center. The constant distance is called the radius.

Ellipse: r = 1 + 0.4 cos(0)

A ellipse is smooth, closed curve made up of all the points in a plane whose combined distance from 2 points, the foci, is always the same.

Spiral: r = 0.2 + 0/(2pi)

A spiral is a curve that winds around a central point while gradually moving farther away from it. A spiral has no sharp turns and continues winding outward.
